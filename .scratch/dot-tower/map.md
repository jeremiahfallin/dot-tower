# dot-tower — Wayfinder Map

`wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A **vertical-slice design spec** for dot-tower — one tower, three climber types, one hero, a closed idle loop with floor locking and prestige — plus the Bevy architecture decisions that are expensive to reverse: the simulation boundary, the save schema, and config-driven tuning. The slice is proven when it runs on desktop **and on a physical Android device**.

This map is done when nothing is left to decide before someone sits down and builds that slice.

## Notes

**Domain.** A pixel-art idle tower-climbing auto-battler in Bevy, in the lineage of [Dot Heroes Ⅱ: Nonstop RPG](https://apkcombo.com/dot-heroes-ii-nonstop-rpg/com.mrgames.summonstower/) (six authored towers, positional heroes, four skills each, gold-cost seals, PvP ranking tower) and [Thousand Floors](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4794380/Thousand_Floors/) (endless floors, idle summons, mystic-arts tree, milestone relics, rebirth). Thousand Floors sits at **42% positive**, and the diagnosis driving this project is that its failures are *bugs and illegibility* — a player prestiging without knowing what it bought, units clipping through walls — not shallow mechanics.

**Skills every session should consult.** `grilling` and `domain-modeling` by default. `prototype` for any ticket typed `prototype`. `research` for any ticket typed `research`.

**Standing preferences for this effort.**

- **Plan, don't do.** Tickets resolve decisions. The pull to start building is the signal the map is finished.
- **Legibility has veto power.** No mechanic ships without its feedback. The player must always be able to answer *what did that just do for me*. This is the project's reason to exist; it does not get cut under schedule pressure.
- **Premium, no microtransactions.** Build a game worth playing, sell it later.
- **guild-forge is a quarry, not a foundation.** Mine `/Users/tacit/dev/guild-forge/guild_forge_gdd.md` for document structure and its crate list. Do **not** inherit its simulation strategy — accelerated logic-only ticking is O(time × floors) and cannot survive an overnight Android suspension.
- **Tuning constants live in hot-reloadable RON, never in Rust.** An idle game is 90% tuning.
- **No custom `Material2d`.** It crashes on Adreno GPUs — the majority Android GPU — in both 0.18 and 0.19 (#22925, open, no fix in flight). Use a custom render pipeline where a material would be the obvious reach.
- **No `Interaction`-based UI.** All UI input goes through `bevy_picking` observers. Bevy's legacy `ui_focus_system` aggregates touches globally rather than per finger (#11553), which breaks multi-touch — and the four hero ability buttons are a multi-touch surface.
- **Vocabulary is fixed.** See `CONTEXT.md`. Retired synonyms: *seal*, *lockdown*, *level* (for floors), *summon*, *unit*.

**Settled at charting.** Premises established during the charting grill. These are not ticket resolutions — they are the ground the tickets stand on.

- Spine: Thousand Floors' infinite generated floors, with a positional hero grafted on.
- Platforms: desktop and Android. iOS and console are out.
- The hero is **stationed on a floor it repeats across**; the player chooses which hero and fires its abilities manually. Four abilities, cooldown-gated, no mana or energy resource.
- The hero is a **throttle**, not a wall-breaker — but *placement* is not the throttle. *Amended by ticket 16: "below the wall it farms, at the wall it pushes" is retired; stationing at the wall dominates on gold and experience alike, because depth beats throughput. Placement is a learnable rule — put the hero where the fighting is. The throttle is **uptime**, and the decisions are which hero and when its abilities fire.*
- Climbers are disposable and anonymous as individuals; the **climber type** carries identity. Three types in the slice — melee, ranged, healer — with data keyed by type from day one.
- **Rank** is the upgrade axis for climbers. Hero levels and climber ranks both reset on prestige.
- **Lock** costs gold, sets the spawn floor, is permanent within a run, and is undone by prestige. Every 10 floors. *Amended by ticket 06: it yields no meaningful passive income — locking is a spawn-point and travel-time mechanic, not an economic pillar.*
- Prestige grants a **permanent multiplier** on health and damage — the single permanent power axis. It must present an explicit before/after. *Amended by ticket 08: the cumulative multiplier is never shown at all — it appears only as **head start** in floors, which is an exact restatement rather than a translation ([ADR 0007](../../docs/adr/0007-prestige-is-shown-as-floors.md)). The multiplier a single prestige earns is a different, small number, and that one is shown.*
- **Relics modify, prestige multiplies.** Relics grant qualitative effects (income, lock cost, cooldowns, spawn rate) and may favour specific climber types. No relic reads "+15% damage". *Amended by ticket 11: income and lock cost are the wrong axes — the curve erases a flat percentage there (`+15%` income is worth 1.54 floors), so those effects must take conditional form ([ADR 0009](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)). A **type-specific** stat relic is now permitted where it is large enough to move composition ([ADR 0003 amendment](../../docs/adr/0003-relics-modify-prestige-multiplies.md)), and relics have **ranks**, bought with a currency prestige grants.*
- Offline gold yields **gold only**, closed-form, capped. The climb does not advance while away. *Amended by ticket 07: the source is not sealed floors but the active band's **best rate** — a per-run high-water mark of rolling 60s gold/sec — and the cap is fixed at 12 hours at 100% of that rate.*
- Save is hand-rolled `serde` to **RON**, one autosave slot plus a rotating backup. No `bevy_save` while still learning Bevy.
- **No persisted RNG state, no seeded determinism.** See `docs/adr/0001`.
- Art runs on placeholder primitives until the loop is proven, then an asset pack, then custom.

## Decisions so far

- [Bevy 0.18 Android: build toolchain and lifecycle](issues/01-bevy-android-support.md) — `cargo-ndk` + Gradle with `GameActivity` is the only maintained path; the surface is destroyed and recreated on suspend and Bevy already handles it; `AppLifecycle::Suspended` is a real synchronous save hook but `WillSuspend`/`WillResume` are never delivered; touch is `WindowEvent::Touch` only, never mouse; `features = ["2d"]` is already Android-complete; custom `Material2d` crashes on Adreno GPUs (#22925).
- [Portrait-first UI approach in Bevy 0.18](issues/02-portrait-ui-approach.md) — built-in `bevy_ui` + `experimental_bevy_ui_widgets` driven through `bevy_picking` observers, which handle touch per-finger natively; `bevy_ui` is already compiled in via `features = ["2d"]`; `Interaction`/`InteractionPalette` is **banned** (global-aggregate touch bug #11553, the pattern guild-forge uses); layout branches on aspect ratio, never `target_os`; `UiAntiAlias::Off` + `Msaa::Off` + `FontSmoothing::None` from day one.
- [Target Bevy 0.18 or 0.19?](issues/13-bevy-version-target.md) — **0.19.1**, decided by #14710 (bevy_ui Android flicker) being fixed transitively via wgpu 29, which 0.18 pins too old to receive and can never be backported. #22925 (Adreno `Material2d` crash) is unfixed in both. Build config is `features = ["2d", "ui", "ui_picking", "android-game-activity"]` — on 0.19 the `2d` collection silently no longer implies `ui` or the Android backend, and `ui` does not imply `ui_picking` (corrected by ticket 04; without it no UI input works at all). Bevy 0.19.1 requires Rust 1.95.
- [The simulation boundary](issues/03-simulation-boundary.md) — two regions: sealed floors are frozen and emit nothing (*the frozen gold rate this originally carried was withdrawn by ticket 06 and replaced as an offline source by ticket 07*), the active band is uncapped with floors activating lazily as climbers approach. No agreement contract exists — locking is a one-way conversion, and carries no gold across the seam. Positions are continuous inside a clamped floor-local space, making wall-escape unrepresentable. `FixedUpdate` at 10Hz. Cleared floors repopulate on a timer (which *is* the farming mechanic); failed floors reset immediately, so attrition can't beat a wall.
- [Climber lifetime and the healer](issues/05-climber-lifetime-and-healer.md) — climbers persist **until death** with **no HP regeneration**, which makes the Wall emergent rather than declared. No party; the individual is the unit of survival. The healer is a **pure-support aura**, stacking linearly, targeting built as a swappable policy. The hero has HP, dies, and respawns **on its stationed floor**, so the throttle is *uptime*. Population cap is **per climber type**, so composition is contested in gold, not slots. Deaths are legible in aggregate at the wall, not per-unit.
- [Progression curve, first pass](issues/06-progression-curve-first-pass.md) — the first-pass constants, in RON. Enemy health and damage ×1.075/floor against gold ×1.095, which makes deeper floors strictly more gold-efficient and is the engine pulling the player upward; past ~1.12 gold the economy runs away. Healer targeting switches from the aura to **single-target, lowest percentage** — the aura fully out-healed the climb, so climbers reached the wall at 100% and the Wall was declared rather than emergent. **Prestige compounds** (a best-ever-floor multiplier dead-ends at floor ~220), and must be shown as *floors of head start*, never as ×10¹¹. Replacement rate, not the respawn timer, is the run-length dial. f64 holds to ~floor 2,000. Runs land in a 30–60 minute band; floor 1,000 arrives around run 10.
- [What the hero contributes that climbers cannot](issues/16-hero-contribution.md) — **the hero multiplies what climbers do, never adds to it** ([ADR 0005](../../docs/adr/0005-the-hero-multiplies-climbers-add.md)). One universal **aura** on its own floor multiplying climber damage and gold; a ×2 aura buys ~+21 floors where every additive hero tried bought +2, and the gain is sub-linear, so it shifts a wall and can never break one. **Taunt is per-hero**, paying for itself only on a tanky one — which is what makes the roster differ in simulation and delivers the escort feeling. The hero levels on **experience from kills in its aura**, never gold, so the two economies never touch. Aura multiplier is relic-only. Ticket 05's uptime claim was backwards: the wall is *safer* without taunt, because the crowd is armour. The slice ships the tank.
- [How the player reads the hero](issues/09-hero-placement-legibility.md) — **the tower says where and now; the strip says how it has been going.** Everything spatial is diegetic — the aura is a lit floor with its multiplier called out, the hero carries a draining health ring and a respawn countdown, experience fills it from the bottom, the wall is hatched. Everything temporal is one strip above the ability bar: a 45s holding sparkline and a record of ability presses with a plain-language verdict on the last. Nothing duplicated between them. Surfaced: the aura row *is* the wall row in the common case, so they need one combined treatment rather than two decorations. Ticket 16's wrong-moment test is satisfiable in a single line of copy.
- [Offline gold formula and the return-screen contract](issues/07-offline-return-contract.md) — offline gold = **best rate × min(elapsed, 12h)**, where best rate is a high-water mark of rolling 60s gold/sec **this run**, reset on prestige. The source moved from sealed floors to the active band: a player who quits at the wall leaves a system whose steady state *is* gold-without-progress. A high-water mark closes ticket 06's measurement trap structurally and lets the formula reference the wall without naming it. **Measured: gold converts to floors logarithmically** — 96× the away time buys ~3× the floors — which deletes the efficiency factor and the decay curve rather than tuning them, and makes the cap an expectation-setting device. Return report is a transient banner in ticket 09's strip, comparator in **ranks**, thresholded on duration never amount. Runs span many pickups and survive closing in full; the active band does not. [ADR 0006](../../docs/adr/0006-offline-gold-pays-the-best-rate.md) supersedes 0002.
- [The prestige before/after screen](issues/08-prestige-before-after.md) — an **itemised ledger, shown before committing, every time**, with the losses named at their real values (`melee rank 56 → 1`) rather than softened into minutes-to-recover. The gain is two numbers: the multiplier **this run earns** (×5.18 at floor 147, still only ×38.6 at floor 1,000 — log-ish in peak floor, so legible forever) and the cumulative total as **head start in floors**, which is exact, never a translation ([ADR 0007](../../docs/adr/0007-prestige-is-shown-as-floors.md)). Measured: head start settles at **~26% of peak floor** and stays there, so the promise never needs restating. A **one-time explainer** precedes the first ledger — the project's one tutorial, earned because the first prestige is the only moment with no baseline. Q4 answers itself: head start's before-value *is* last prestige's result, so nothing needs remembering. The bigger finding lands elsewhere — **prestige is a stall decision, not a multiplier decision** (one more floor buys +0.084 floors of head start against 22.8 for prestiging, and that margin never narrows), which makes the trigger the risk rather than the screen, and opens ticket 18.

- [Portrait layout and the desktop frame](issues/10-portrait-layout-desktop-frame.md) — **modality is priced by frequency** ([ADR 0008](../../docs/adr/0008-spending-is-never-modal.md)). Ranks and locks are permanently on screen because they are bought every few seconds; relics and prestige may be modal because they are rare. Measured, and it decides the ticket: **the tower column does not want the height a full-frame layout gives it** — climbers pile at the wall, so one row carries the fighting and the interesting band is bounded by the lock interval. **12–14 floors is the whole game**, so the permanent spend block costs nothing real. The camera follows the highest floor reached, clamped at the lock line, and in a mature run the two clamps meet and **it stops moving entirely**. The lock keeps its diegetic button on the tower, being the one purchase whose effect is spatial. Desktop gets the *same* column, strip and ability bar at the same size plus one panel holding what the phone reaches modally — **nothing is visible on desktop that a phone player cannot reach**, which killed the rail variant's run ledger as ticket 09's rejected second temporal surface. Nothing may ever occlude the ability bar.

- [Relic effect vocabulary](issues/11-relic-effect-vocabulary.md) — **the vocabulary is a seam, not a list**: effect *kinds* are a closed Rust enum, *relics* are open RON data as `(condition, effect, magnitude)`, so the catalogue grows without the vocabulary growing. Measured, and it sets the shape: **a flat percentage on an axis the curve already drives is invisible** — `+15%` income is 1.54 floors, `−20%` lock cost is 1.6, `−20%` rank cost is 0.85 of a rank, and forty stacked income relics move `gold_base` 1.0950 → 1.0970, far under the 1.12 runaway. Not dangerous, *beneath the noise floor*, which is worse ([ADR 0009](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)). The axes that survive are flat-in-depth — and those are exactly the ones that **saturate**, so flat-axis levers are few and substantial while catalogue growth comes from conditionals. Relics are **granted at first-time floor milestones**, unique, permanent, one per axis so stacking never arises, and carry **ranks** bought with a currency prestige grants on **peak floor beyond your previous best** — chosen structurally to kill prestige-spam without the run-length measurement ticket 17 still owes. `+15%` melee health is **exactly 1.0 ranks**, which is what fixes the line for type-stat relics ([ADR 0003 amended](../../docs/adr/0003-relics-modify-prestige-multiplies.md)). [ADR 0008](../../docs/adr/0008-spending-is-never-modal.md) **holds** — spending is a once-per-run burst at prestige — unless currency ever becomes earnable mid-run, which reopens ticket 10. Ranged reach turned out to be baseline combat identity that does not exist yet, split out as ticket 19.

- [Ranged attack reach](issues/19-ranged-attack-reach.md) — **no reach; combat is floor-local and threat weighting is the abstraction** ([ADR 0010](../../docs/adr/0010-combat-is-floor-local-and-has-no-reach.md)), with the arrow as sprite flavour. Three mechanics get called reach and all three were measured, after a first pass had to be thrown out: variants that *grind* rather than stall run 4× as long and reach a deeper floor for that reason alone, which made front-to-back targeting look like +29 floors until every run was pinned to identical wall-clock time, at which point it reversed. **Attack reach is a damage buff in a positional costume** — ±2 buys 2.22× gold, and doubling ranged damage buys 2.01×. **Standoff works and costs** — ranged deaths 172 → 114, peak floor −15.1% at depth, and it breaks even only by widening ticket 16's ±0 aura, so positional safety for ranged is a *hero* decision. **Formation ordering is indistinguishable from randomising it**: melee front 811, healer front 812, no order at all 815. The bigger finding kills the premise — **ranged is not safer to begin with.** Melee dies 1.08× as often per capita; with threat weighting off entirely, 0.97×; with ranged made untouchable, 1.03×. **Per-type replacement sets the death rate** and compresses a 10× health swing into 1.3× of survival, so no climber type has a survival identity and the cap-and-interval machinery is why — appended to ticket 15. Ticket 03's floor-reset rule turns out never to have been in the model (impact 147 → 144, inside the noise).

- [Climber composition and the per-type caps](issues/15-climber-composition.md) — **composition is authored, not chosen** ([ADR 0011](../../docs/adr/0011-composition-is-authored.md)). The game has four decisions, not five. Measured across eleven splits of a fixed 90-climber budget at identical wall-clock time, the spread is 22.1% in run 1 and **0.7% at depth** — composition matters while the player is weak and stops mattering once they are strong, so as a decision it would be one that *expires*. Checked against the suspicion that this was the lock curve's fault: fixing the lock curve makes composition **more** inert, not less. Gold competition does not bite either — buy-cheapest, buy-even and all-in-on-one-type span 140 to 148, because rank cost forces all three to be bought regardless of intent. Each type still earns its place (removing one costs 12–20%), and what they are *for* is **legibility**: the tower has to read as an army fighting. Settled: caps are never bought with gold; **one global replacement interval plus an authored ratio** replaces three caps and three intervals, decoupling the ratio from ticket 06's run-length dial and ticket 19's survival dial; the spawn is skipped at the cap; the ratio is authored to read as an army rather than tuned to the optimum; the mix is never a number on screen. `type cap` is **retired** as a relic effect kind — worth 0.0% for melee and ranged at ×3 the cap ([ADR 0009 amended](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)) — which leaves ticket 11's type affinity with neither axis intact. Adds **Composition** and **Replacement** to `CONTEXT.md` and rewrites **Type cap**. Opened tickets 20 and 21.

## Not yet specified

In scope, but not yet sharp enough to ticket. Graduates as the frontier advances.

- **Hero roster beyond the slice.** Ticket 16 settled the *shape*: heroes share one universal aura and differ by abilities, health, and whether they taunt — with tank, fighter, mage and cleric named as the intended roles and the tank shipping in the slice. What remains is how many exist, how they are unlocked, and what each one's four abilities actually do. Waits on the tank proving the aura-and-uptime loop in play.
- **Enemy and floor variety.** What actually lives on floor 400 that didn't live on floor 40, and whether floors have visual or mechanical themes. Hangs on the curve.
- **Setting, tone, and narrative framing.** Thousand Floors leans on the Classic of Mountains and Seas. dot-tower has no setting yet. Not urgent, but it shapes art direction.
- **Art pipeline past placeholder.** Palette, sprite dimensions, animation budget, whether to buy a pack or commission. Waits on the loop being proven fun.
- **Exposing healer targeting to the player.** Ticket 06 built the policy seam and settled single-target lowest-percentage as the shipped default, so what remains is whether the player ever chooses — a standing order set per climber type — and whether a second policy earns its place. Waits on watching the shipped one in play.
- **A run-against-run comparison surface.** Ticket 08 built and rejected a floors-against-minutes chart showing the run just played against the projected next one, with the real line drawn over the projection on the following run. It shows the stall rather than stating it and answers *was it worth it* as a picture. Rejected for the slice as a second temporal surface competing with ticket 09's strip — not as a bad idea. Waits on ticket 18 settling what the strip has to carry, and on ticket 17 saying whether a run is even a unit the player perceives at depth.
- **The word for the floor the climb has reached.** Tickets 03, 06 and 10 all lean on *frontier*, and it is not a glossary term. It is not a synonym for the **wall** — they coincide only once the climb has stalled, which is most of the time but not the interesting part. Ticket 10 declined to invent it while resolving a layout question. Whoever next writes about the moving edge of the climb should settle it.
- **The first-pass relic catalogue, and what type affinity even is now.** Ticket 11 settled the structure — roughly 8–12 milestone relics weighted to flat-axis levers early with a conditional tail — but not which ones, and its two type-affinity axes have both since fallen. Ticket 19 killed reach as an axis outright ([ADR 0010](../../docs/adr/0010-combat-is-floor-local-and-has-no-reach.md)) and narrowed `type stat` to damage, since a survivability relic is beneath the noise floor when replacement compresses a 10× health swing into 1.3× of survival. Ticket 15 then retired `type cap` entirely, at 0.0% for melee and ranged ([ADR 0009 amended](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)). So *what a relic that favours ranged climbers actually does* is now the open question, upstream of the catalogue itself. Both wait on ticket 20 to say what the late game is made of.
- **The name of the currency prestige grants.** Ticket 11 declined to invent it: *vestige* and its neighbours read as near-synonyms of *relic*, and *ascension* is already retired. Same shape of gap as the word for the moving edge of the climb, above. Whoever writes the prestige screen copy should settle both.
- **Audio.** Entirely unexamined.
- **Accessibility beyond the climber types.** Text legibility on a portrait phone, and whatever else surfaces once the UI is real. The climber-type half of this graduated into ticket 21 — ADR 0011 made the tower column the *only* representation of the mix, so telling the three types apart stopped being a nicety.
- **Balance methodology past the first curve.** How tuning gets validated — headless sim runs, telemetry, playtesting. Ticket 06's model is the obvious seed; whether it becomes a kept tool or stays throwaway is unresolved. Ticket 07 leaned on it a second time and hit its limits: the stall heuristic is too noisy to measure a run-length *trend*, which is now ticket 17's problem too. Ticket 19 hit it a third time and harder — the heuristic did not merely add noise, it **inverted a headline result**, because a variant that grinds rather than stalls buys depth from run length alone; pinning every run to identical wall-clock time is now the only comparison the model can be trusted for. It also found the model has never implemented ticket 03's rule that a floor nobody is fighting restores its pack (impact on the baseline 147 → 144, inside the noise, so nothing is invalidated). Both point the same way: if this model becomes a kept tool it needs to be checked *against the decisions it is used to defend*.
- **Steam release mechanics.** Store page, pricing, wishlists, build pipeline.
- **The composition ratio and the cap ceilings, as integers.** ADR 0011 settled that the ratio is authored to read as an army rather than tuned — near-equal measures best, and an army that is one-third medics does not read like one — but the numbers themselves are authoring, and they wait on ticket 20. Population is spawn rate times lifetime and lifetime is mostly walking, so how big the crowd is and how far it walks are the same question, and ticket 20 owns it.

## Out of scope

Ruled beyond this destination. Never graduates; returns only if the destination is redrawn as a fresh effort.

- **PvP / Ranking Tower** — Dot Heroes II's competitive ladder. Beyond a single-player vertical slice, and it drags in backend infrastructure.
- **Microtransactions and any IAP economy** — ruled out by the project's premise.
- **Live-ops, seasons, battle passes, daily login economies.**
- **iOS and console ports.**
- **Multiplayer or co-op of any kind.**
- **Extracting shared idle/simulation machinery into a crate with guild-forge** — abstraction fitted to one use case. Revisit only if a third project appears.
