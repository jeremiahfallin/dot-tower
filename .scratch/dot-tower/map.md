# dot-tower — Wayfinder Map

`wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A **vertical-slice design spec** for dot-tower — one tower, three climber types, one hero, a closed idle loop with floor locking and prestige — plus the Bevy architecture decisions that are expensive to reverse: the simulation boundary, the save schema, and config-driven tuning. The slice is proven when it runs on desktop **and on a physical Android device**.

This map is done when nothing is left to decide before someone sits down and builds that slice.

## Notes

**Domain.** A pixel-art idle tower-climbing auto-battler in Bevy, in the lineage of [Dot Heroes Ⅱ: Nonstop RPG](https://apkcombo.com/dot-heroes-ii-nonstop-rpg/com.mrgames.summonstower/) (six authored towers, positional heroes, four skills each, gold-cost seals, PvP ranking tower) and [Thousand Floors](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4794380/Thousand_Floors/) (endless floors, idle summons, mystic-arts tree, milestone relics, rebirth). Thousand Floors sits at **42% positive**, and the diagnosis driving this project is that its failures are *bugs and illegibility* — a player prestiging without knowing what it bought, units clipping through walls — not shallow mechanics.

**Skills every session should consult.** `grilling` and `domain-modeling` by default. `prototype` for any ticket typed `prototype`. `research` for any ticket typed `research`.

**Standing preferences for this effort.**

- **Plan, don't do.** Tickets resolve decisions. The pull to start building is the signal the map is finished.
- **Legibility has veto power.** No mechanic ships without its feedback. The player must always be able to answer *what did that just do for me*. This is the project's reason to exist; it does not get cut under schedule pressure.
- **Premium, no microtransactions.** Build a game worth playing, sell it later.
- **guild-forge is a quarry, not a foundation.** Mine `/Users/tacit/dev/guild-forge/guild_forge_gdd.md` for document structure and its crate list. Do **not** inherit its simulation strategy — accelerated logic-only ticking is O(time × floors) and cannot survive an overnight Android suspension.
- **Tuning constants live in hot-reloadable RON, never in Rust.** An idle game is 90% tuning.
- **No custom `Material2d`.** It crashes on Adreno GPUs — the majority Android GPU — in both 0.18 and 0.19 (#22925, open, no fix in flight). Use a custom render pipeline where a material would be the obvious reach.
- **No `Interaction`-based UI.** All UI input goes through `bevy_picking` observers. Bevy's legacy `ui_focus_system` aggregates touches globally rather than per finger (#11553), which breaks multi-touch — and the four hero ability buttons are a multi-touch surface.
- **Vocabulary is fixed.** See `CONTEXT.md`. Retired synonyms: *seal*, *lockdown*, *level* (for floors), *summon*, *unit*.

**Parked (2026-09-08).** The device-bound line — [ticket 22](issues/22-bevy-ui-android-rendering.md)'s remaining verdicts and [ticket 14](issues/14-android-performance-budget.md)'s supply half — is deliberately parked until the desk work finishes. Everything left on the map (17, 18, 20, 21) needs no hardware. Parking defers the proof, it does not redraw it: the destination still reads desktop *and* a physical Android device, and the question of whether that sentence changes comes to a head when the map is otherwise done — ideally priced by then by ticket 22's borrowed-Adreno reading, the cheapest test on the board. The Pixel 11 launch abort found on the way is [ticket 24](issues/24-pixel11-launch-abort.md), independent of the parking.

**Settled at charting.** Premises established during the charting grill. These are not ticket resolutions — they are the ground the tickets stand on.

- Spine: Thousand Floors' infinite generated floors, with a positional hero grafted on.
- Platforms: desktop and Android. iOS and console are out.
- The hero is **stationed on a floor it repeats across**; the player chooses which hero and fires its abilities manually. Four abilities, cooldown-gated, no mana or energy resource.
- The hero is a **throttle**, not a wall-breaker — but *placement* is not the throttle. *Amended by ticket 16: "below the wall it farms, at the wall it pushes" is retired; stationing at the wall dominates on gold and experience alike, because depth beats throughput. Placement is a learnable rule — put the hero where the fighting is. The throttle is **uptime**, and the decisions are which hero and when its abilities fire.*
- Climbers are disposable and anonymous as individuals; the **climber type** carries identity. Three types in the slice — melee, ranged, healer — with data keyed by type from day one.
- **Rank** is the upgrade axis for climbers. Hero levels and climber ranks both reset on prestige.
- **Lock** costs gold, sets the spawn floor, is permanent within a run, and is undone by prestige. Every 10 floors. *Amended by ticket 06: it yields no meaningful passive income — locking is a spawn-point and travel-time mechanic, not an economic pillar. Amended by ticket 20: the cost is K seconds of rolling income, not a gold magnitude — no geometric base can hold a constant relationship to income — and locks below the account's best-ever floor are free ([ADR 0013](../../docs/adr/0013-locks-are-priced-in-time.md)).*
- Prestige grants a **permanent multiplier** on health and damage — the single permanent power axis. It must present an explicit before/after. *Amended by ticket 08: the cumulative multiplier is never shown at all — it appears only as **head start** in floors, which is an exact restatement rather than a translation ([ADR 0007](../../docs/adr/0007-prestige-is-shown-as-floors.md)). The multiplier a single prestige earns is a different, small number, and that one is shown.*
- **Relics modify, prestige multiplies.** Relics grant qualitative effects (income, lock cost, cooldowns, spawn rate) and may favour specific climber types. No relic reads "+15% damage". *Amended by ticket 11: income and lock cost are the wrong axes — the curve erases a flat percentage there (`+15%` income is worth 1.54 floors), so those effects must take conditional form ([ADR 0009](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)). A **type-specific** stat relic is now permitted where it is large enough to move composition ([ADR 0003 amendment](../../docs/adr/0003-relics-modify-prestige-multiplies.md)), and relics have **ranks**, bought with a currency prestige grants.*
- Offline gold yields **gold only**, closed-form, capped. The climb does not advance while away. *Amended by ticket 07: the source is not sealed floors but the active band's **best rate** — a per-run high-water mark of rolling 60s gold/sec — and the cap is fixed at 12 hours at 100% of that rate.*
- Save is hand-rolled `serde` to **RON**, one autosave slot plus a rotating backup. No `bevy_save` while still learning Bevy.
- **No persisted RNG state, no seeded determinism.** See `docs/adr/0001`.
- Art runs on placeholder primitives until the loop is proven, then an asset pack, then custom.

## Decisions so far

- [Bevy 0.18 Android: build toolchain and lifecycle](issues/01-bevy-android-support.md) — `cargo-ndk` + Gradle with `GameActivity` is the only maintained path; the surface is destroyed and recreated on suspend and Bevy already handles it; `AppLifecycle::Suspended` is a real synchronous save hook but `WillSuspend`/`WillResume` are never delivered; touch is `WindowEvent::Touch` only, never mouse; `features = ["2d"]` is already Android-complete; custom `Material2d` crashes on Adreno GPUs (#22925).
- [Portrait-first UI approach in Bevy 0.18](issues/02-portrait-ui-approach.md) — built-in `bevy_ui` + `experimental_bevy_ui_widgets` driven through `bevy_picking` observers, which handle touch per-finger natively; `bevy_ui` is already compiled in via `features = ["2d"]`; `Interaction`/`InteractionPalette` is **banned** (global-aggregate touch bug #11553, the pattern guild-forge uses); layout branches on aspect ratio, never `target_os`; `UiAntiAlias::Off` + `Msaa::Off` + `FontSmoothing::None` from day one.
- [Target Bevy 0.18 or 0.19?](issues/13-bevy-version-target.md) — **0.19.1**, decided by #14710 (bevy_ui Android flicker) being fixed transitively via wgpu 29, which 0.18 pins too old to receive and can never be backported. #22925 (Adreno `Material2d` crash) is unfixed in both. Build config is `features = ["2d", "ui", "ui_picking", "android-game-activity"]` — on 0.19 the `2d` collection silently no longer implies `ui` or the Android backend, and `ui` does not imply `ui_picking` (corrected by ticket 04; without it no UI input works at all). Bevy 0.19.1 requires Rust 1.95.
- [The simulation boundary](issues/03-simulation-boundary.md) — two regions: sealed floors are frozen and emit nothing (*the frozen gold rate this originally carried was withdrawn by ticket 06 and replaced as an offline source by ticket 07*), the active band is uncapped with floors activating lazily as climbers approach. No agreement contract exists — locking is a one-way conversion, and carries no gold across the seam. Positions are continuous inside a clamped floor-local space, making wall-escape unrepresentable. `FixedUpdate` at 10Hz. Cleared floors repopulate on a timer (which *is* the farming mechanic); failed floors reset immediately, so attrition can't beat a wall.
- [Climber lifetime and the healer](issues/05-climber-lifetime-and-healer.md) — climbers persist **until death** with **no HP regeneration**, which makes the Wall emergent rather than declared. No party; the individual is the unit of survival. The healer is a **pure-support aura**, stacking linearly, targeting built as a swappable policy. The hero has HP, dies, and respawns **on its stationed floor**, so the throttle is *uptime*. Population cap is **per climber type**, so composition is contested in gold, not slots. Deaths are legible in aggregate at the wall, not per-unit.
- [Progression curve, first pass](issues/06-progression-curve-first-pass.md) — the first-pass constants, in RON. Enemy health and damage ×1.075/floor against gold ×1.095, which makes deeper floors strictly more gold-efficient and is the engine pulling the player upward; past ~1.12 gold the economy runs away. Healer targeting switches from the aura to **single-target, lowest percentage** — the aura fully out-healed the climb, so climbers reached the wall at 100% and the Wall was declared rather than emergent. **Prestige compounds** (a best-ever-floor multiplier dead-ends at floor ~220), and must be shown as *floors of head start*, never as ×10¹¹. Replacement rate, not the respawn timer, is the run-length dial. f64 holds to ~floor 2,000. Runs land in a 30–60 minute band; floor 1,000 arrives around run 10. *Amended by ticket 20: under time-priced locks the band holds for the first ~3 runs only, floor 1,000 arrives at run 7, and mature runs no longer end on their own — the run-length dial is now ticket 17's whole question. Amended by ticket 17: with the earned multiplier paying for new territory only, run length becomes a growth curve — ~40 min early, 2.5–3 h at maturity — and floor 1,000 arrives at run 16; "replacement is the run-length dial" is retired in favour of the re-conquest walk.*
- [What the hero contributes that climbers cannot](issues/16-hero-contribution.md) — **the hero multiplies what climbers do, never adds to it** ([ADR 0005](../../docs/adr/0005-the-hero-multiplies-climbers-add.md)). One universal **aura** on its own floor multiplying climber damage and gold; a ×2 aura buys ~+21 floors where every additive hero tried bought +2, and the gain is sub-linear, so it shifts a wall and can never break one. **Taunt is per-hero**, paying for itself only on a tanky one — which is what makes the roster differ in simulation and delivers the escort feeling. The hero levels on **experience from kills in its aura**, never gold, so the two economies never touch. Aura multiplier is relic-only. Ticket 05's uptime claim was backwards: the wall is *safer* without taunt, because the crowd is armour. The slice ships the tank.
- [How the player reads the hero](issues/09-hero-placement-legibility.md) — **the tower says where and now; the strip says how it has been going.** Everything spatial is diegetic — the aura is a lit floor with its multiplier called out, the hero carries a draining health ring and a respawn countdown, experience fills it from the bottom, the wall is hatched. Everything temporal is one strip above the ability bar: a 45s holding sparkline and a record of ability presses with a plain-language verdict on the last. Nothing duplicated between them. *Amended by ticket 18: the strip carries one more temporal fact — the run's running earned-state (new floors, ×earned), the mirror of the record line on the column.* Surfaced: the aura row *is* the wall row in the common case, so they need one combined treatment rather than two decorations. Ticket 16's wrong-moment test is satisfiable in a single line of copy.
- [Offline gold formula and the return-screen contract](issues/07-offline-return-contract.md) — offline gold = **best rate × min(elapsed, 12h)**, where best rate is a high-water mark of rolling 60s gold/sec **this run**, reset on prestige. The source moved from sealed floors to the active band: a player who quits at the wall leaves a system whose steady state *is* gold-without-progress. A high-water mark closes ticket 06's measurement trap structurally and lets the formula reference the wall without naming it. **Measured: gold converts to floors logarithmically** — 96× the away time buys ~3× the floors — which deletes the efficiency factor and the decay curve rather than tuning them, and makes the cap an expectation-setting device. Return report is a transient banner in ticket 09's strip, comparator in **ranks**, thresholded on duration never amount. Runs span many pickups and survive closing in full; the active band does not. [ADR 0006](../../docs/adr/0006-offline-gold-pays-the-best-rate.md) supersedes 0002.
- [The prestige before/after screen](issues/08-prestige-before-after.md) — an **itemised ledger, shown before committing, every time**, with the losses named at their real values (`melee rank 56 → 1`) rather than softened into minutes-to-recover. The gain is two numbers: the multiplier **this run earns** (×5.18 at floor 147, still only ×38.6 at floor 1,000 — log-ish in peak floor, so legible forever) and the cumulative total as **head start in floors**, which is exact, never a translation ([ADR 0007](../../docs/adr/0007-prestige-is-shown-as-floors.md)). Measured: head start settles at **~26% of peak floor** and stays there, so the promise never needs restating. A **one-time explainer** precedes the first ledger — the project's one tutorial, earned because the first prestige is the only moment with no baseline. Q4 answers itself: head start's before-value *is* last prestige's result, so nothing needs remembering. The bigger finding lands elsewhere — **prestige is a stall decision, not a multiplier decision** (one more floor buys +0.084 floors of head start against 22.8 for prestiging, and that margin never narrows), which makes the trigger the risk rather than the screen, and opens ticket 18. *Amended by ticket 17: at maturity head start settles at ~21% of peak (the multiplier now pays for new territory only), and the decision is no longer at the stall — prestiging before passing the account's best earns nothing, just past it is optimal, waiting for the stall is 1.5× slower. The trigger becomes the past-your-best moment, which ticket 18 must mark.*

- [Portrait layout and the desktop frame](issues/10-portrait-layout-desktop-frame.md) — **modality is priced by frequency** ([ADR 0008](../../docs/adr/0008-spending-is-never-modal.md)). Ranks and locks are permanently on screen because they are bought every few seconds; relics and prestige may be modal because they are rare. Measured, and it decides the ticket: **the tower column does not want the height a full-frame layout gives it** — climbers pile at the wall, so one row carries the fighting and the interesting band is bounded by the lock interval. **12–14 floors is the whole game**, so the permanent spend block costs nothing real. The camera follows the highest floor reached, clamped at the lock line, and in a mature run the two clamps meet and **it stops moving entirely**. The lock keeps its diegetic button on the tower, being the one purchase whose effect is spatial. Desktop gets the *same* column, strip and ability bar at the same size plus one panel holding what the phone reaches modally — **nothing is visible on desktop that a phone player cannot reach**, which killed the rail variant's run ledger as ticket 09's rejected second temporal surface. Nothing may ever occlude the ability bar.

- [Relic effect vocabulary](issues/11-relic-effect-vocabulary.md) — **the vocabulary is a seam, not a list**: effect *kinds* are a closed Rust enum, *relics* are open RON data as `(condition, effect, magnitude)`, so the catalogue grows without the vocabulary growing. Measured, and it sets the shape: **a flat percentage on an axis the curve already drives is invisible** — `+15%` income is 1.54 floors, `−20%` lock cost is 1.6, `−20%` rank cost is 0.85 of a rank, and forty stacked income relics move `gold_base` 1.0950 → 1.0970, far under the 1.12 runaway. Not dangerous, *beneath the noise floor*, which is worse ([ADR 0009](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)). The axes that survive are flat-in-depth — and those are exactly the ones that **saturate**, so flat-axis levers are few and substantial while catalogue growth comes from conditionals. Relics are **granted at first-time floor milestones**, unique, permanent, one per axis so stacking never arises, and carry **ranks** bought with a currency prestige grants on **peak floor beyond your previous best** — chosen structurally to kill prestige-spam without the run-length measurement ticket 17 still owes. *Amended by tickets 17 and 18: the measurement exists now, and the multiplier pays on the same basis ([ADR 0014](../../docs/adr/0014-the-multiplier-pays-for-new-territory.md)) — the currency no longer carries the anti-spam burden alone, and its gain line stays on the prestige ledger only.* `+15%` melee health is **exactly 1.0 ranks**, which is what fixes the line for type-stat relics ([ADR 0003 amended](../../docs/adr/0003-relics-modify-prestige-multiplies.md)). [ADR 0008](../../docs/adr/0008-spending-is-never-modal.md) **holds** — spending is a once-per-run burst at prestige — unless currency ever becomes earnable mid-run, which reopens ticket 10. Ranged reach turned out to be baseline combat identity that does not exist yet, split out as ticket 19.

- [Ranged attack reach](issues/19-ranged-attack-reach.md) — **no reach; combat is floor-local and threat weighting is the abstraction** ([ADR 0010](../../docs/adr/0010-combat-is-floor-local-and-has-no-reach.md)), with the arrow as sprite flavour. Three mechanics get called reach and all three were measured, after a first pass had to be thrown out: variants that *grind* rather than stall run 4× as long and reach a deeper floor for that reason alone, which made front-to-back targeting look like +29 floors until every run was pinned to identical wall-clock time, at which point it reversed. **Attack reach is a damage buff in a positional costume** — ±2 buys 2.22× gold, and doubling ranged damage buys 2.01×. **Standoff works and costs** — ranged deaths 172 → 114, peak floor −15.1% at depth, and it breaks even only by widening ticket 16's ±0 aura, so positional safety for ranged is a *hero* decision. **Formation ordering is indistinguishable from randomising it**: melee front 811, healer front 812, no order at all 815. The bigger finding kills the premise — **ranged is not safer to begin with.** Melee dies 1.08× as often per capita; with threat weighting off entirely, 0.97×; with ranged made untouchable, 1.03×. **Per-type replacement sets the death rate** and compresses a 10× health swing into 1.3× of survival, so no climber type has a survival identity and the cap-and-interval machinery is why — appended to ticket 15. Ticket 03's floor-reset rule turns out never to have been in the model (impact 147 → 144, inside the noise).

- [Climber composition and the per-type caps](issues/15-climber-composition.md) — **composition is authored, not chosen** ([ADR 0011](../../docs/adr/0011-composition-is-authored.md)). The game has four decisions, not five. Measured across eleven splits of a fixed 90-climber budget at identical wall-clock time, the spread is 22.1% in run 1 and **0.7% at depth** — composition matters while the player is weak and stops mattering once they are strong, so as a decision it would be one that *expires*. Checked against the suspicion that this was the lock curve's fault: fixing the lock curve makes composition **more** inert, not less. Gold competition does not bite either — buy-cheapest, buy-even and all-in-on-one-type span 140 to 148, because rank cost forces all three to be bought regardless of intent. Each type still earns its place (removing one costs 12–20%), and what they are *for* is **legibility**: the tower has to read as an army fighting. Settled: caps are never bought with gold; **one global replacement interval plus an authored ratio** replaces three caps and three intervals, decoupling the ratio from ticket 06's run-length dial and ticket 19's survival dial; the spawn is skipped at the cap; the ratio is authored to read as an army rather than tuned to the optimum; the mix is never a number on screen. `type cap` is **retired** as a relic effect kind — worth 0.0% for melee and ranged at ×3 the cap ([ADR 0009 amended](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)) — which leaves ticket 11's type affinity with neither axis intact. Adds **Composition** and **Replacement** to `CONTEXT.md` and rewrites **Type cap**. Opened tickets 20 and 21.

- [Save schema and migration](issues/12-save-schema.md) — the save is **two structs mirroring exactly what prestige resets** — `Account` and `Run` — so prestige becomes `run = Run::default()` and ticket 08's ledger is generated from the same declaration, instead of the same list living in the save, the reset and the ledger and two of them being wrong. Adds **Account** to `CONTEXT.md`. Three decisions turned out to be one: the cumulative multiplier is **stored, not derived** (deriving makes the earned-multiplier formula load-bearing forever, so a later retune silently moves everyone's permanent power), unknown relic ids are **retained verbatim, never pruned** (ADR 0009 makes the catalogue churn by design, and dropping is irreversible), and post-release schema changes **migrate, never reset** — one principle, recorded as [ADR 0012](../../docs/adr/0012-the-save-is-authoritative.md): no code path may retroactively reduce what the save says the player has. The failure paths carried the findings. **The backup rotates on load, not on write** — atomicity already covers process death, so the backup's real job is surviving a bug that writes a valid-but-wrong save, and rotating per write overwrites the good copy within seconds; copying the parsed primary at startup makes it *the state your last session ended in*, and costs no Android frame budget. The copy is **byte-for-byte, never re-serialised**, or it re-stamps the timestamp and erases offline time. **A newer-version save disarms every autosave trigger** — refusing to load is the harmless half; the fresh game plus the 60s timer overwrites the real save within a minute, and that is the *default* behaviour of everything else agreed here. Corruption is never silent (quarantine, restore, and say what was restored *in floors*); `NotFound` branches before any parse, so first launch never apologises. Four triggers, extending ticket 01's two with a 60s timer and prestige as a commit point. Settings live in a **separate file**, or the backup rewinds preferences alongside progress. Cheating posture: nothing — but range validation *for robustness*, since one `NaN` propagates through every `f64` multiplier with no crash to point at.

- [Bevy hello-world on a physical Android device](issues/04-android-device-bringup.md) — **it runs.** `cargo-ndk` + Gradle/`GameActivity`, Rust 1.97.1, NDK 30, sdk 36, Gradle wrapper pinned to 8.14.3 (AGP 8.13.2 cannot run on Gradle 9.7.1); Pixel 10 Pro, Android 17/API 37, **Tensor G5 / PowerVR**, Vulkan. **Probe E closes ticket 01's inference on hardware**: `AppLifecycle::Suspended` is a real synchronous save hook, the write plus `sync_all()` took **2 ms**, and `WillSuspend` is never delivered — ticket 12's design stands. Four build-chain defects fixed, none of them Bevy or the NDK, all of them the kind that only appear on a real machine. **But `bevy_ui` does not render on this device** — sprite layer correct, UI layer scattered and frame-varying, silent (no wgpu error, no validation failure); proven to be neither the harness (renders on macOS/Metal) nor ticket 02's pixel-art render settings (reverting them changes nothing). That took probes B, C and D down with it and is split out as [ticket 22](issues/22-bevy-ui-android-rendering.md) (prototype, inheriting every gated reading including the two ticket 02 added) and [ticket 23](issues/23-upstream-bevy-ui-android.md) (research, upstream half, runs in parallel). Ticket 22 now owns ticket 13's *reason* — 0.19.1 was chosen because #14710 was fixed via wgpu 29, and that fix is unobservable here — and the UI foundation in tickets 02 and 10, neither invalidated yet. Standing regardless of how it resolves: winit **never delivers** inset or content-rect changes on Android (`TODO` in the source), so ticket 02's ~40 lines of safe-area handling must **poll** `content_rect()`, never listen. A **Godot probe harness** carrying the same probes now exists at [`prototypes/04-godot-probe/`](prototypes/04-godot-probe/) as a cross-engine control — if it composites a UI layer correctly on the same handset, the device is exonerated and the fault is Bevy's — which makes it ticket 22's cheapest first cut. The engine choice itself is open and deliberately not front-run: no ADR and no other ticket turns on it.

- [Upstream state of bevy_ui on Android](issues/23-upstream-bevy-ui-android.md) — **#14710 is real, verified in every link, and is a different bug from ours.** wgpu#8853 was a *missing pipeline barrier between two render passes writing the same colour attachment*; every device in its 35-comment thread is Mali or MediaTek/Exynos, the symptom is uniformly *flicker*, and both working mitigations are pass-composition remedies. A barrier bug decides whether the UI pass **lands** — it cannot scramble geometry within the pass or make solid nodes vanish every frame while glyphs partly survive. **#14710 was also never closed** (`state: open`, no Bevy PR has ever referenced it, nobody has re-tested released 0.19 on affected hardware). **PowerVR is a target wgpu has effectively no exposure to**: zero correctness workarounds in the whole tree against named ones for Qualcomm, NVIDIA, Intel and MoltenVK, and `wgpu#7669` (PowerVR crash, `external: driver-bug`) open with zero comments since 2025-05-05 — so the thin layer is **upstream of Bevy**, as ticket 22 suspected it might be. Shipping `bevy_ui` on Android is otherwise the norm (Bevy's own mobile example, the canonical template, nine developers on twelve handsets), so that is not why it fails here. For ticket 22: **"the silence is the tell" is withdrawn** — wgpu loads the validation layer only if present and logs its absence below Bevy's filter, stock retail Android does not ship it, and the modern attribute-alignment VUs are *unimplemented anyway*; and "alignment is the **only** structural difference" was overstated, since the sprite path is `VertexStepMode::Instance` and fetches no per-vertex attribute at all. In exchange the ticket gained what it recorded as missing: **prior art for the class on the exact silicon** — [godot#121005](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/121005), same GPU and driver build `25.1@6794074`, legal-but-unusual vertex attribute state silently mis-fetched, draw disappears entirely, no errors, other draws in the same frame fine. Core Vulkan **explicitly permits** the layout (`VUID-vkCmdDraw-format-10390` requires only component-size alignment; wgpu encodes exactly that), so the hypothesis needs a plain driver conformance failure. Ticket 13 needs **a footnote, not an amendment** — its finding #2 is correct but should lose "the real decider", since 0.18 being a dead branch carries the decision alone.

- [Travel time and the lock curve](issues/20-travel-time-and-the-lock-curve.md) — **a lock costs time, not a gold magnitude, and is free in conquered territory** ([ADR 0013](../../docs/adr/0013-locks-are-priced-in-time.md)). Measured: no geometric base holds a constant cost-to-income relationship, because income compounds faster than per-kill gold — kill rate grows with ranks — so every base is tuned-then-rot (2.478 under-matches, 3.0 over-matches, and the apparent 2.7 match moved the moment sealed income came out). The shipped design: **K = 30 seconds of rolling-60s income** — matched by construction, immune to rank retunes — with locks **below the account's best-ever floor free**; without that rule a mature blitz outruns a 20-floors/min lock line and the road balloons to ~270 floors mid-run. Excavated first: the model had never implemented ticket 06's sealed-floor withdrawal — 0.0% of income under the shipped curve (why it hid) but 99.3% under any fixed curve, so every prior fixed-curve reading, ticket 15's included, was an artifact; now implemented behind `sealedIncome`, defaults byte-identical, ticket 15's conclusions re-verified. Depth is curve-independent (746–789 at run 6 pinned) — **locks shape the stream, they never bought depth**, and the stakes are shape: never locking costs 8–9% of peak but explodes the road to 607 floors with the crowd pinned on the caps. The fog's integers graduate: at replacement 1.67s the crowd runs **~57 fresh / 15–53 at depth**, composition **4/3/2** (26/19/13 early, exactly 24/18/12 at run 11), walk 12–25 floors — inside ticket 10's column window; replacement is the clean crowd dial (7/15/35 across 3.33/1.67/0.83s with walk and peak flat, so ADR 0011's dial works — the inertness ticket 14 measured was the broken curve's), climb speed also dials at ~12% depth cost. **Runs stop ending**: runs 7–10 of a campaign still climb at a 180-minute cap (M 4.6×10¹³, peak 1849) — the lock curve *was* the run-length curve and no longer is, which unblocks ticket 17 with a sharper brief. The prestige curve survives (head start 16% in run 1 then 24–28%, floor 1,000 at run 7); entities max 157 mean 42 — the range ticket 14's parked session should test. Ticket 21's crowd premise is re-numbered on that ticket.

- [Does run length grow as the account matures?](issues/17-run-length-growth.md) — **the multiplier a run earns pays for new territory only** ([ADR 0014](../../docs/adr/0014-the-multiplier-pays-for-new-territory.md)): peak beyond the account's best-ever floor, ×1.00 if the run never passes it. Measured first, and the shipped rule — earned as a function of the run's raw peak — is a **prestige-spam engine**: from a mature state, 5-minute prestige cycles compound to M = 4.2×10¹¹⁰ in 12 hours (ln M at 21.2/h against 2.6 at the stall), because every cycle re-banks a multiplier for territory the account already owned; the runs never even re-reach their best, being walk-limited to ~170 floors. Ticket 11 feared this and priced the *relic currency* on peak-beyond-best to kill it, but the multiplier itself paid for re-conquest — the principle is now uniform: **the account pays for new territory only**, and a fresh account is bit-for-bit unchanged. Under the fixed basis, spam earns literally nothing, and **run length becomes structural and lengthens with maturity** — the map's founding assumption, finally produced rather than assumed: early runs are pure frontier (~40 min), a mature run must first re-conquer a head start that grows with best-ever, so natural runs land at 2.5–3 h and the *optimal* prestige interval drifts upward too (60–70 min at best-ever 412, 70–80 at 771). The stall over-waits by 1.5×, so prestige timing flips from ticket 08's stall decision to a **past-your-best decision** — the moment ticket 18 must mark. The campaign slows in floor terms (floor 1,000 at run 16) with new territory steady at 48–76 floors/run through run 20 — pacing, not a dead-end, and divisor/exponent are tuning data. Offline gold is *confirmed* rather than reopened: away time cannot earn multiplier at all, which is ticket 07's gold-only contract made structural. The instrument problem dissolved: pinned-wall-clock policy sweeps answer run-length questions without ever detecting a stall.

- [How the game says a run is over](issues/18-run-is-over.md) — **the run ends at your own record** ([ADR 0015](../../docs/adr/0015-the-run-ends-at-your-own-record.md)). Grilled after ticket 17 moved the value event: the multiplier pays for new territory only, so a run earns ×1.00 until it passes the account's best-ever floor — an exact, saved fact — and **stall detection retires from the design entirely** (nothing shipped needs it; offline gold already uses best rate; the founding false-negative failure mode is now impossible). The record is a **line on the tower column** ("Record" added to `CONTEXT.md`), in the lock-line/wall-hatch family: before the run passes it, the line is the distance to beat, and on a mature run it is the re-conquest target ticket 17's lengthening runs climb toward — the run-length rhythm given a face. The prestige affordance is **always present and deliberately dumb** — a small permanent control opening ticket 08's modal ledger, never appearing or issuing verdicts; early prestige is self-punishing and self-explaining (the ledger shows ×1.00, nothing gained). Ticket 09's strip gains exactly one line — the run's running earned-state, the temporal mirror of the spatial line. Ticket 11's currency keeps its third gain line on the ledger only; its basis is now identical to the multiplier's (ADR 0014), so the incentive divergence it feared is structurally impossible and the re-check it wanted cost nothing. **The column's visual vocabulary is closed** — lock line, wall hatch, aura, record — which hands ticket 21 a fixed budget.

- [Telling the climber types apart](issues/21-telling-the-types-apart.md) — **type reads as shape, hue, and one tag** ([ADR 0016](../../docs/adr/0016-type-reads-as-shape-hue-and-one-tag.md)). Settled by prototype ([`prototypes/21-types/`](prototypes/21-types/)): four directions × five colour-vision simulations × an authored-resolution ladder, reacted to live. **Shape and hue carry type together** — melee's shield slab, ranged's bow column, healer's hat-and-staff, on a colour-blind-safe palette (blue / ember-orange / pale-and-gold); colour-only dies by construction under deuteranopia, and per-head glyph tags are redundant — they survive in exactly one place, **a small gold cross above the healer alone**, the type ticket 15 measured as the only one with marginal value, legible across the column and still visible at 1× authored pixels. The tag is sprite anatomy, not column furniture, so ADR 0015's four-mark closure holds unamended. **A climber's state never renders**: per-unit health pips were prototyped as the failure case and read as noise, which promotes ticket 05's deaths-in-aggregate to the individual's entire state surface. Size lands exactly in ticket 10's window — **48px floors, 13 in view** — with sprites **authored 16×16 and the 8×8 hand grid as the demonstrated readability floor**. Verification names the art pass's first target: the ranged silhouette reads as a pillar rather than a figure at column scale (ADR 0010's "arrow as sprite flavour" delivered, distinguishable but not body-like), and melee's shield trim can parse as a companion sprite in clusters.

## Not yet specified

In scope, but not yet sharp enough to ticket. Graduates as the frontier advances.

- **Hero roster beyond the slice.** Ticket 16 settled the *shape*: heroes share one universal aura and differ by abilities, health, and whether they taunt — with tank, fighter, mage and cleric named as the intended roles and the tank shipping in the slice. What remains is how many exist, how they are unlocked, and what each one's four abilities actually do. Waits on the tank proving the aura-and-uptime loop in play.
- **Enemy and floor variety.** What actually lives on floor 400 that didn't live on floor 40, and whether floors have visual or mechanical themes. Hangs on the curve.
- **Setting, tone, and narrative framing.** Thousand Floors leans on the Classic of Mountains and Seas. dot-tower has no setting yet. Not urgent, but it shapes art direction.
- **Art pipeline past placeholder.** Palette, sprite dimensions, animation budget, whether to buy a pack or commission. Waits on the loop being proven fun. *Amended by ticket 21: dimensions and palette are now constrained, not open — 16×16 authored grids with the 8×8 hand set as the readability floor, 48px floors, a colour-blind-safe palette ([ADR 0016](../../docs/adr/0016-type-reads-as-shape-hue-and-one-tag.md)) — and the first art-pass target is named: give the ranged silhouette a body. Animation budget and pack-vs-commission still wait on the loop.*
- **Exposing healer targeting to the player.** Ticket 06 built the policy seam and settled single-target lowest-percentage as the shipped default, so what remains is whether the player ever chooses — a standing order set per climber type — and whether a second policy earns its place. Waits on watching the shipped one in play.
- **A run-against-run comparison surface.** Ticket 08 built and rejected a floors-against-minutes chart showing the run just played against the projected next one, with the real line drawn over the projection on the following run. It shows the stall rather than stating it and answers *was it worth it* as a picture. Rejected for the slice as a second temporal surface competing with ticket 09's strip — not as a bad idea. Ticket 17 settled the half it was waiting on: a run *is* a unit at depth (prestige cadence is the session's rhythm, and new-territory-per-run is a concrete anchor), and ticket 18 settled the strip's load (it gained one line, the run's earned-state). What remains is only the post-slice question of whether the comparison earns its surface once the slice is playable.
- **The word for the floor the climb has reached.** Tickets 03, 06 and 10 all lean on *frontier*, and it is not a glossary term. It is not a synonym for the **wall** — they coincide only once the climb has stalled, which is most of the time but not the interesting part. Ticket 10 declined to invent it while resolving a layout question. Whoever next writes about the moving edge of the climb should settle it.
- **The first-pass relic catalogue, and what type affinity even is now.** Ticket 11 settled the structure — roughly 8–12 milestone relics weighted to flat-axis levers early with a conditional tail — but not which ones, and its two type-affinity axes have both since fallen. Ticket 19 killed reach as an axis outright ([ADR 0010](../../docs/adr/0010-combat-is-floor-local-and-has-no-reach.md)) and narrowed `type stat` to damage, since a survivability relic is beneath the noise floor when replacement compresses a 10× health swing into 1.3× of survival. Ticket 15 then retired `type cap` entirely, at 0.0% for melee and ranged ([ADR 0009 amended](../../docs/adr/0009-relic-effects-avoid-axes-the-curve-erases.md)). So *what a relic that favours ranged climbers actually does* is now the open question, upstream of the catalogue itself. Ticket 20 has said what the late game is made of — locks priced in time, the road bounded to the column, the crowd a replacement dial — and ticket 17 has said what ends a run (passing the account's best; [ADR 0014](../../docs/adr/0014-the-multiplier-pays-for-new-territory.md) makes the multiplier pay for new territory only, so any relic touching prestige timing designs against the past-your-best moment).
- **The name of the currency prestige grants.** Ticket 11 declined to invent it: *vestige* and its neighbours read as near-synonyms of *relic*, and *ascension* is already retired. Same shape of gap as the word for the moving edge of the climb, above. Whoever writes the prestige screen copy should settle both.
- **Audio.** Entirely unexamined.
- **What `settings.ron` actually contains.** Ticket 12 settled that settings live in their own file, separate from the save, with no migration chain — but not what is in it. Overlaps audio and the accessibility item below, and waits on the UI being real.
- **Accessibility beyond the climber types.** Text legibility on a portrait phone, and whatever else surfaces once the UI is real. The climber-type half of this graduated into ticket 21 and is now settled there — shape + hue + healer tag, no per-climber state ([ADR 0016](../../docs/adr/0016-type-reads-as-shape-hue-and-one-tag.md)) — including the standing obligation that the art pass re-runs the prototype's colour-vision simulations against final assets.
- **Balance methodology past the first curve.** How tuning gets validated — headless sim runs, telemetry, playtesting. Ticket 06's model is the obvious seed; whether it becomes a kept tool or stays throwaway is unresolved. Ticket 07 leaned on it a second time and hit its limits: the stall heuristic is too noisy to measure a run-length *trend*, which is now ticket 17's problem too. Ticket 19 hit it a third time and harder — the heuristic did not merely add noise, it **inverted a headline result**, because a variant that grinds rather than stalls buys depth from run length alone; pinning every run to identical wall-clock time is now the only comparison the model can be trusted for. It also found the model has never implemented ticket 03's rule that a floor nobody is fighting restores its pack (impact on the baseline 147 → 144, inside the noise, so nothing is invalidated). Ticket 20 hit it a fourth time and quietest: the model had silently never implemented ticket 06's sealed-floor withdrawal, and because it measured 0.0% under the shipped curve it hid for fourteen tickets — every fixed-curve reading until then was riding it. All four point the same way: if this model becomes a kept tool it needs to be checked *against the decisions it is used to defend*.
- **Steam release mechanics.** Store page, pricing, wishlists, build pipeline.
