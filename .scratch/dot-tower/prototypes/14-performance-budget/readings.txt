=== 3. Why a thin spread is free ===
Ticket 14 Q2 worried that if climbers spread thin instead of clumping,
activation churn would be much higher than predicted. They DO spread
thin. Here is what the active floors actually contain.

worst sample of a deep run (floor 611):
  climbers spread over        181 floors
  floors active (spread +-2)   62
  of those, CONTESTED         4
  enemy entities              86  (1.39 per active floor, pack size 4)
  climber entities            89
  TOTAL                       176

An active floor holds far less than a full pack because trailing
climbers walk through ground the leaders already cleared. Spread costs
activation events, not entities -- and the events are cheap.

=== 4. What sets the crowd, and does the ceiling bind? ===
The model now runs ticket 15's mechanism: ONE global replacement
interval against an authored ratio, with per-type caps surviving only as
a ceiling that ADR 0011 says should never bind. So `replacement` is the
crowd dial, not the caps -- and whether the ceiling binds is testable.

replacement  crowd  entities(max)  active(max)  churn/s  capSkips/sample
       0.50     88            151           59     1.59            17.22
       1.00     88            167           76     2.02             7.84
       1.67     88            176           93     2.04             3.90
       3.00     87            210          138     2.87             1.28
       5.00     81            205          160     2.50             0.09

--- and the ceiling, under both lock curves ---
Ticket 15 predicted the cap "stops binding entirely" under the FIXED lock
curve (lockCostBase 2.5), which is ticket 20's open work.

lock curve      run    peak   crowd   m/r/h     capSkips/sample
default (4.0)  run 1     147      82 37/27/18            2.90
default (4.0)  run 6     750      87 39/29/19            4.15
FIXED (2.5)    run 1     142      34  15/11/8            0.00
FIXED (2.5)    run 6    1220       9    4/3/2            0.00

ADR 0011 is right, and it is CONDITIONAL on the lock curve. Under the
default curve the ceiling binds constantly and the crowd is pinned at 90.
Under the fixed curve it never binds once and the live mix settles on the
authored ratio exactly -- which is what "authored" is supposed to mean.
