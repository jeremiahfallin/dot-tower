=== 3. Why a thin spread is free ===
Ticket 14 Q2 worried that if climbers spread thin instead of clumping,
activation churn would be much higher than predicted. They DO spread
thin. Here is what the active floors actually contain.

worst sample of a deep run (floor 646):
  climbers spread over        212 floors
  floors active (spread +-2)   82
  of those, CONTESTED         7
  enemy entities              114  (1.39 per active floor, pack size 4)
  climber entities            90
  TOTAL                       205

An active floor holds far less than a full pack because trailing
climbers walk through ground the leaders already cleared. Spread costs
activation events, not entities -- and the events are cheap.

=== 4. How hard is this pinned to the crowd size? ===
Population sits at exactly 90 in every run above -- the sum of the three
per-type caps. That mechanism is GONE: ticket 15 replaced three caps and
three intervals with one global replacement interval against an authored
ratio, and how big the crowd actually is belongs to ticket 20, still open.
So the number that matters is not 90, it is the slope.

crowd  entities(max)  enemies  active(max)  churn/s
   90            205      114          110     2.52
  180            270       90          168     2.11
  360            366       30          176     1.43
  720            436       30          177     1.49
 1440            436       30          177     1.49

It SATURATES. Raising the ceiling past ~360 changes nothing: 720 and
1440 give identical runs, because population is spawn rate times lifetime
and the ceiling has stopped binding. That is CONTEXT.md's claim about the
type cap -- "under a healthy lock curve it does not bind at all" -- shown
directly, and it is false at the CURRENT value of 90, where population sits
pinned at the ceiling in every run, and true from roughly 360 up.

The number ticket 20 needs: left to spawn interval and lifetime alone, the
crowd settles near 405 climbers and the whole simulation costs ~436
entities -- still under 40% of the 1,100 prediction with no ceiling at all.
